ABOUT HAVENRIDGE BUILD

Professional Building Standards & Code-Compliant Construction

Havenridge Build is a full-service design-build general contractor serving Cambridge, Kitchener, Waterloo, Guelph, and surrounding communities. Since 2014, we have helped homeowners improve their homes through carefully planned kitchens, bathrooms, basements, additions, and complete home renovations.

Led by founder and owner Micheal Smith, our team coordinates the entire renovation process—from initial planning, 3D architectural scope, and municipal permits through trade management, bench-crafted construction, and final completion. Our clients receive clear communication, organized daily project updates through the Buildern client portal, and one accountable team throughout their renovation.

As a certified Baeumler Approved and RenoMark renovator, we are committed to professional standards, code-compliant construction, and quality craftsmanship. Our work is backed by a two-year warranty covering structural defects and water penetration, plus a one-year workmanship warranty.
